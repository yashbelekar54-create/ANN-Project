# Dataset card

- Profiles: 650
- Job roles: 20
- Annotation rows: 13000
- Positive labels: 933
- Source: generated locally for this educational project; not a public real-resume dataset.
- Annotation method: deterministic seeded simulation based on a primary role plus role-skill overlap.
- Personal data: none; names are fictional sample names.
- Intended use: demonstrating multi-label preprocessing, ANN training and recommendation workflows.
- Not intended for: employment decisions, hiring, candidate rejection or claims about labour-market accuracy.
